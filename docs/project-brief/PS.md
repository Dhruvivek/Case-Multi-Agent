# AI Mystery Detective Team

## Problem Statement

A Single AI Assistant may rush to a conclusion, overlook evidence, repeat assumptions, or sound confident without enough proof.

## The Challenge

- Complex Evidence
- Conflicting Clues
- Uncertain Conclusions

## Our Goal

Build A Begineer-friendly multi-agent system where specialized AI Detectives analyze a fictional mystery,, share findings through an orchestration workflowm challenge weak assumptions, and produce an evidence-based verdict for human review.
